# xAI IPC: Hardware Prototypes & Network Topologies

Here are the visual prototypes for the xAI Intelligent Power Core panels, designed for different target environments, as well as an architectural diagram of the autonomous microgrid.

## 1. Residential: The Advanced 'White' Prototype
Designed for modern homes, this highly advanced sleek edge-to-edge glass panel acts as the central intelligence node for the home's energy routing and altruistic compute. 

Key features of this upgraded prototype:
* **Thermal Management:** The front cover functions as a high-tech small heat exchanger with visible cooling fins.
* **Energy Storage & EV Routing:** It physically links with an adjacent Tesla Powerwall and features dedicated side-mounted heavy-duty DC terminals for Solar In and EV Charging, along with a specialized Optimus humanoid robot charging port.
* **Smart UI & AI Integration:** The center is dominated by a massive oversized glass HMI touch screen display with a sleek pop-out physical keyboard. At the very top bezel is a built-in AI camera lens and speaker grille array for two-way communication.
* **Safety & Telemetry:** Smart solid-state breakers inside feature green and yellow glowing indicator lights, as well as physical red high-contrast LOTO emergency trip levers. 
* **Altruistic Compute Engine:** Nestled right beside the breakers is the glowing, liquid-cooled Edge TPU compute blade module running the federated AI loads.
* **Connectivity & Sensory Input:** Includes I/O terminals (HDMI, Ethernet, Coax) at the bottom alongside a dedicated row of high-tech circular multipin sensor ports with glowing LED rings. It networks directly with a nearby Starlink satellite antenna/router and a distinct 5G mmWave mesh antenna for microgrid fallback communication.
